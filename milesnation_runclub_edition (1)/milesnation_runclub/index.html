Good to know

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before choosing your challenge.

Is this a physical event?
No. milesnation challenges are virtual. You complete your distance on your own route and schedule, without travelling to an event venue.
When can I complete it?
Complete your selected distance within the challenge window shown on its card. You can choose when and where to exercise during that period, following the challenge rules.
What proof do I submit?
Submit an activity screenshot or record that clearly shows your completed distance and relevant activity details. Use the submission method and instructions provided after registration. Your submission must meet the selected challenge's verification requirements.
When does my medal ship?
For registrations that include a medal, shipping is expected within {{SHIPPING_DAYS}} after your activity is approved. Delivery varies by location. Make sure your shipping address and phone number are accurate.
